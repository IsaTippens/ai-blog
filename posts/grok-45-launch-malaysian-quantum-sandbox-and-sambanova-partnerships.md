### 1. Grok 4.5 Launches with Aggressive Code Generation Price-Slashing

Accelerating the trend of autonomous software systems, [IBM has announced a major upgrade to its Bob agentic development platform](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-09-ibm-advances-enterprise-ai-software-development-with-multi-agent-capabilities-and-specialized-modernization-workflows). ### 3. Meta to Construct Massive Multi-Billion Dollar Canadian AI Data Center
Scaling its compute footprint outside the United States, [Meta has unveiled plans to invest billions to construct its first AI data center in Canada](https://www.wsls.com/business/2026/07/08/meta-plans-billions-for-first-ai-data-center-in-canada-largest-outside-the-us/). Located in Alberta, the facility will become Meta's largest infrastructure footprint outside of the US, leveraging the region's cold climate and clean hydro-energy resources to support next-generation training clusters and high-density liquid-cooled hardware.

## III. FINTECH INFRASTRUCTURE & BANKING

### 6. J.P. Morgan Chase Selects SambaNova Systems for Enterprise AI Inference

### 7. MAS Proposes CIS Code Amendments to Fast-Track Innovative Fund Types
Singapore's financial regulator, the [Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS), has published a landmark consultation paper](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/mas-proposes-regulatory-changes-to-facilitate-faster-approvals-of-new-fund-types) seeking industry feedback on streamlining approvals for innovative retail fund structures. MAS proposes introducing a dedicated "Alternative Funds Appendix" to the Code on Collective Investment Schemes, enabling faster offer authorizations for algorithmic trading, tokenized assets, and green finance investments while maintaining strict retail consumer guardrails.
