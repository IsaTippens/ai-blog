## I. TELECOM INFRASTRUCTURE & AI RADIO NETWORKS

### 1. NTT DOCOMO and Samsung Electronics Validate AI-Based Baseband Optimization

Scaling national quantum-safe infrastructure, [the University of Calgary launched a dedicated Quantum Defence Innovation Hub](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/quantum-defence-innovation-ucalgary-hub/).
