## I. FRONTIER SAFETY & INTERNATIONAL PACING MECHANISMS

### 1. Over 1,100 AI Researchers Sign Open Letter Calling for International Pacing Frameworks
Highlighting safety concerns among foundational developers, [more than 1,100 AI researchers across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta signed an open letter](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-29-2026). In a landmark development for AI-driven security research, [Anthropic's Mythos frontier model successfully identified algorithmic weaknesses in HAWK and AES encryption variants](https://securityboulevard.com/2026/07/mythos-found-weaknesses-in-quantum-resistant-encryption/).
