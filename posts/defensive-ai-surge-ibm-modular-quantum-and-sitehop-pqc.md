## II. MODULAR QUANTUM ARCHITECTURES & ALGORITHMIC GAINS

### 4. IBM Links First Modular Cryogenic Systems for Multi-Chip Quantum Scaling
Overcoming physical dilution refrigerator constraints, [IBM announced the successful integration of its first modular cryogenic systems linking multi-chip quantum processors](https://thequbitreport.com/the-qubit-report-week-ending-8-29-2026/). The cryogenic interconnect architecture allows quantum processors to scale across separate modular dilution units toward fault-tolerant logical qubit operations.

### 5. Chinese Academy of Sciences Unveils Quantum Algorithm for Directed Graph Analysis
Advancing topological quantum computing, [researchers at the Chinese Academy of Sciences demonstrated a quantum algorithm in Quantum Science and Technology delivering exponential speedups for analyzing directed graph networks](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/chinese-sciences-quantum-algorithm-speeds-up/). The algorithm utilizes path homology to analyze complex directional relationships in telecommunications and financial routing graphs.

### 6. Diraq Opens First U.S. Quantum Laboratory in Chicago for Silicon Spin Qubits
Scaling CMOS-compatible quantum processors, [Australian quantum developer Diraq officially opened its first U.S. quantum laboratory in Chicago](https://thequbitreport.com/the-qubit-report-week-ending-8-29-2026/). The facility accelerates commercial manufacturing of silicon spin qubits leveraging existing commercial semiconductor foundry processes to target million-qubit QPUs.
