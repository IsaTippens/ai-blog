## I. CRITICAL CYBERSECURITY THREATS & AI INFRASTRUCTURE

### 1. CISA and CSA Alert: Active Exploitation of Critical RCE Flaw in IBM Langflow (CVE-2026-9198)
In an emergency security advisory, [Singapore's Cyber Security Agency (CSA) and US CISA warned of active zero-day exploitation targeting IBM Langflow OSS](https://www.csa.gov.sg/alerts-and-advisories/alerts/al-2026-099/). Designated as CVE-2026-9198 with a maximum CVSS v3.1 severity score of **9.8**, the code injection flaw allows unauthenticated threat actors to achieve full Remote Code Execution (RCE) on default Langflow deployments. Development teams utilizing the low-code agent framework are urged to upgrade to version 1.11.0 immediately.

### 2. INC Ransomware Dominates Exploitation of SonicWall SMA 1000 Gateway Flaws
Sustaining perimeter attacks, [threat intelligence from Resecurity confirms that INC Ransomware has accelerated attacks against SonicWall SMA 1000 series appliances](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/inc-ransomware-emerges-as-dominant.html) by chaining CVE-2026-15409 and CVE-2026-15410. Organizations are instructed to audit remote access logs and enforce multi-factor authentication.
