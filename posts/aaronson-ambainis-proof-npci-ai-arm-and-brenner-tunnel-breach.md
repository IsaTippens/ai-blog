Utilizing an automated reasoning pipeline guided by a non-public Anthropic model, the system synthesized a complete proof that was independently checked and formally accepted by the Lean interactive theorem prover on its third day of computation, followed by two subsequent formal refinements.

The conjecture, originally posed by Scott Aaronson and Andris Ambainis, establishes a universal boundary on quantum speedups by analyzing how low-degree polynomials behave under single-bit input flips. Researchers demonstrated that an ordinary classical computer can accurately predict the output of any quantum algorithm—up to an epsilon error margin—for nearly all possible inputs, provided the algorithm's representing polynomial has limited degree. By replacing a previously unproven inequality with a novel determinant-based formulation over affine switch settings, the team proved that quantum computers cannot achieve exponential speedup on unstructured yes-or-no queries unless the input string exhibits rare, highly structured global symmetries.

### 4\. Brenner Base Tunnel SE Confirms Critical European Transit Data Breach

Exposing the vulnerability of strategic civil engineering mega-projects, [Brenner Base Tunnel SE confirmed that unauthorized threat actors breached internal corporate networks](https://www.scworld.com/brief/brenner-base-tunnel-se-confirms-data-breach-as-everest-group-claims-theft) ([Laptop Breach Advisory Visual](https://www.scworld.com/api/thumbor-proxy?width=1920&src=https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.cyberriskalliance.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2025%2F10%2F102125_laptop_breach.jpg)).
