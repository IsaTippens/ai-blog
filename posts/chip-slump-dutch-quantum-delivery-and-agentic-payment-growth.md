## II. SOVEREIGN QUANTUM BOUNDARIES

### 2. Quix Quantum Delivers First Complete Dutch Quantum Computer to Germany's DLR
Marking a massive milestone for physical European hardware development, [University of Twente spin-off Quix Quantum has delivered the first complete quantum computer built by a Dutch company](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2026/07/first-dutch-quantum-computer-comes-with-message-for-the-hague/). The machine was handed over to the German Aerospace Center (DLR) after almost four years of development. However, the milestone came with a warning: Quix's leadership urged the Dutch government to accelerate public procurement, national investments, and industrial deployment to prevent the Netherlands from losing its exceptional quantum capabilities to international competitors.

### 6. California's Copyright Protection Bill AB 412 Gaining State Senate Momentum
Enforcing strict transparency standards on foundational AI training datasets, [California's landmark copyright protection bill AB 412 is gaining heavy legislative momentum](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/ai-legislative-update-july17-2026) in the State Senate. The bill would require commercial AI developers to document and register all copyrighted training materials and establish secure, auditable query channels for rights owners.
