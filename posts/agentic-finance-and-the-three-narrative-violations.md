## I. AGENTIC FINANCE & COMPLIANCE

### 1. MAS and BuildFin.ai Publish SAFR White Paper for Autonomous Finance
In a major milestone for decentralized financial safety, the [Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS)](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/mas-partners-industry-to-develop-safeguards-for-ai-agents-in-finance), alongside leading global banks, has published a comprehensive industry white paper titled **“Safeguards for Agentic Finance at Runtime (SAFR)”**. Developed under MAS' BuildFin.ai initiative, SAFR establishes a standardized compliance framework containing real-time governance checkpoints to verify, validate, and record an AI agent's actions before execution. This ensures autonomous agents operate safely within pre-defined bank mandates and risk boundaries without requiring manual human intervention.

### 2. The Great AI Squeeze: Three Core Market Beliefs Break
Market analysts are reporting that three massive, widely-held consensus beliefs about the artificial intelligence trade [completely broke this week](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yFEOnBT0Hgw):
*   **The Model Monopoly**: The assumption that "you must own the foundation model" to capture value, as hyper-specialized vertical applications and low-cost open-source models commoditize raw models.
*   **Infinite Capex Tolerance**: The belief that Wall Street would fund astronomical infrastructure capex indefinitely without demanding near-term cash-flow monetization.
*   **The "Magnificent Seven" Hegemony**: The expectation that the largest tech giants would grow without friction, as capital begins rotating toward businesses that can prove immediate returns, wiping out over $2.3 trillion in tech valuations this month.

## II. TECH POLICY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

### 3. California's AI Copyright Bill AB 412 Advances to Senate
The California State Legislature is moving aggressively to enforce AI training data transparency. Draft bill [AB 412](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/ai-legislative-update-july3-2026) (the AI Copyright Protection Act) has successfully passed the Assembly and won approvals from the Senate Judiciary Committee (6-2) and the Appropriations Committee. The bill mandates that all commercial AI developers document any copyrighted materials used in their training sets and establish a mandatory reporting mechanism allowing rights owners to audit and query their intellectual property usage.

### 4. Signal and Telegram Face Username Scrutiny from the Indian Government
The Indian Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology has officially [sent regulatory notices to secure messaging networks Signal and Telegram](techpp.com/2026/07/03/the-daily-brief-july-3-2026-samsung-z-flip-8/). The notices demand strict compliance audits surrounding their public username structures, end-to-end data localization, and national sovereignty protocols.
