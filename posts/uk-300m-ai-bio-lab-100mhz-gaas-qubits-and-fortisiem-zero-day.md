## II. QUANTUM HARDWARE DESIGN & CODE BOUNDARY AUTOMATION

### 4\. Triple-Dot GaAs Semiconductor Architecture Accelerates Qubit Speeds Past 100 MHz

Overcoming physical gate-speed bottlenecks in solid-state quantum computing, [physicists demonstrated a triple-quantum-dot architecture in gallium arsenide (GaAs) that boosts exchange gate speeds beyond 100 MHz](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/gaas-triple-dot-qubit-speed-design/). By tuning inter-dot tunneling barriers, the design drastically shortens two-qubit gate operations while preserving electron spin coherence, providing a viable path toward dense, microsecond-scale semiconductor quantum processors.
