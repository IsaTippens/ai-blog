## II. SEMICONDUCTOR MEGAPROJECTS & HARDWARE ACQUISITIONS

### 3. Nvidia and SK Group Partner on $500 Billion AI Infrastructure Initiative
Shoring up next-generation memory and compute supply lines, [Nvidia and South Korea's SK Group announced a joint $500 billion initiative](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2026/07/25/nvidia-and-sk-group-scale-up-ai-infrastructure-with-us500-billion-initiative/). The megaproject focuses on ramping up production of High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM4) chips and building state-of-the-art AI data center hubs across Asia-Pacific.

### 4. Intel Raises 2026 Capex to $20 Billion as Enterprise CPU AI Demand Surges
Signaling positive momentum in its hardware turnaround, [Intel raised its 2026 capital expenditure guidance to $20 billion](https://virginiabusiness.com/intel-raises-capital-spending-ai-chip-demand/). CEO Lip-Bu Tan confirmed that growing enterprise adoption of Intel's data center CPUs for AI infrastructure workloads drove Q3 revenue forecasts above Wall Street estimates.

### 5. Microchip Technology Signs Definitive Agreement to Acquire Edge AI Pioneer Hailo
Expanding its embedded computing footprint, [Microchip Technology (NASDAQ: MCHP) signed a definitive agreement to acquire Israeli edge AI processor developer Hailo](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/en/news/6407986). The acquisition integrates Hailo's vision processors, robotics accelerators, and edge software pipelines directly into Microchip's microcontroller ecosystem.

The framework defines unified operational resilience and threat auditing standards for Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs) operating within the EU.
