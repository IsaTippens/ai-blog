## II. ZERO-TRUST IDENTITY & CONFIDENTIAL COMPUTING

### 4. Microsoft Makes Passkeys Default in Entra ID, Retires SMS and Voice Auth
Eliminating credential-harvesting vulnerabilities, [Microsoft established FIDO2 passkeys as the mandatory default authentication method across Entra ID](https://gbhackers.com/). The policy update formally phases out SMS and voice-based one-time passwords (OTPs) to neutralize adversary SIM-swapping and real-time AI voice-cloning bypasses.

### 5. Google Releases Open-Source Toolkit to Execute AI on Encrypted Data
Advancing privacy-preserving machine learning, [Google open-sourced software tools allowing AI models to compute directly on encrypted data in hardware enclaves](https://northeasttimes.com/2026/08/15/google-releases-open-source-tool-to-run-ai-on-encrypted-data/). By combining Confidential Computing with zero-knowledge attestation, the toolkit allows enterprises to query proprietary models without exposing raw plaintext to cloud operators.
