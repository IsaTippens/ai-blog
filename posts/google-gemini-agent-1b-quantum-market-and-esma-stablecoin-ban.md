## III. FINTECH REGULATION, STABLECOINS & BANKING PLUMBING

### 5\. ESMA Issues Emergency Directive Mandating January 2027 Stablecoin Phaseout

Delivering a structural regulatory shock to European digital asset markets, [the European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) issued a binding directive on October 8](https://www.onesafe.io/blog/eu-stablecoin-regulation-2027-deadline). The ruling mandates that all crypto-asset service providers (CASPs) operating within the EU must completely eliminate exposure to non-compliant stablecoins by January 8, 2027\. Going beyond earlier spot trading restrictions under MiCA, the mandate enforces an outright prohibition on custody, transfers, advisory services, and portfolio management, forcing institutional treasuries and DeFi liquidity providers to transition reserves exclusively into licensed electronic money tokens (EMTs).

### 6\. Bahrain FinTech Bay Expands AI Strategy for Regional Financial Institutions

Accelerating Middle Eastern banking modernization, [Bahrain FinTech Bay unveiled an expanded artificial intelligence strategy](https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/08/bahrain-fintech-bay-expands-strategy-to-develop-ai-solutions-for-financial-institutions/). The initiative establishes dedicated engineering sandboxes for regional commercial banks to pilot machine-learning credit underwriting, automated fraud anomaly detection, and algorithmic compliance monitoring.
