## II. PRIVILEGED ACCESS SECURITY & PERIMETER ADVISORIES

### 3\. Fortra Patches Critical Flaws in BoKS Server Security Suite

Securing enterprise privileged access management (PAM) controllers, [Fortra released urgent patches for multiple critical vulnerabilities in BoKS Server Security](https://www.securityweek.com/fortra-patches-critical-vulnerabilities-in-boks/). The security flaws allowed unauthenticated remote attackers to bypass identity verification, corrupt heap memory, and execute arbitrary root shell commands on administrative proxy nodes controlling access to corporate server infrastructure.

### 4\. doxx.net Secures \$38M to Build Protective Guardrails for Autonomous AI Agents

Tackling the emerging attack surface created by web-browsing software agents, [doxx.net announced a \$38 million funding round to prevent enterprise AI agent misadventures](https://www.securityweek.com/doxx-net-raises-38-million-to-prevent-ai-agent-on-the-internet-misadventures/). The company's platform provides real-time isolation sandboxes and credential redaction filters that stop autonomous agents from accidentally leaking credentials, falling victim to prompt injection, or executing unauthorized financial transactions on the public internet.

### 5\. Citrix NetScaler Post-Patch Firmware Instability Triggers Reboots

Following emergency updates for zero-days CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772, [Cyber Security News reported that enterprise administrators are encountering continuous reboot loops on patched NetScaler appliances](https://cybersecuritynews.com/citrix-netscaler-0-day-patch/). The crashes, primarily occurring during high-throughput TLS decryption cycles, have complicated remediation efforts across global data centers already contending with [federal remediation mandates](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/01/new-cisco-sd-wan-zero-day-exploited-in-the-wild-cve-2026-76504/).

## III. POST-QUANTUM CRYPTOGRAPHY & QUANTUM MATERIALS

The integration protects private keys and asset transfers for LINE NEXT's Unifi Wallet against future quantum decryption attacks, establishing a production benchmark for lattice-based cryptography on consumer mobile devices.

### 7\. Manchester Leads £12.6M MEAD Consortium to Synthesize Quantum Materials

Advancing atomic-precision quantum hardware manufacturing, [the University of Manchester announced the £12.6 million Molecular Engineering for Atomic Devices (MEAD) initiative](https://thequbitreport.com/the-qubit-report-week-ending-10-3-2026/). The research consortium coordinates European physicists and materials scientists to assemble scalable quantum components atom by atom, addressing coherence limitations in next-generation solid-state qubit platforms.

### 8\. Splendor Labs Unveils Hardware Quantum Security Card for Banking

Strengthening physical payment credentials, [Splendor Labs released a dedicated Quantum Security Card integrating embedded Quantum Random Number Generators (QRNG)](https://thequbitreport.com/the-qubit-report-week-ending-10-3-2026/). The card embeds NIST-standardized post-quantum signature algorithms into tamper-resistant secure elements, providing quantum-safe physical identity verification for institutional banking and government facilities.
