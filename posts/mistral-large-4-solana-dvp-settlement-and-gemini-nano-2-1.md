## II. INSTITUTIONAL FINTECH, ATOMIC SETTLEMENT & DIGITAL ASSETS

### 4\. Solana Foundation Launches Open-Source Solana DvP Settlement with J.P. Morgan Input

Bridging traditional financial market plumbing with high-throughput public blockchains, [the Solana Foundation launched Solana DvP on October 6](https://cryptorank.io/ru/news/feed/6f1f0-solana-foundation-launches-dvp-settlement-program-with-j-p-morgan-input). Released under the MIT open-source license, the program provides a standardized, audited atomic delivery-versus-payment (DvP) settlement rail that eliminates traditional multi-day clearing delays, allowing banks, custodians, and exchanges to settle tokenized securities against digital cash with second-level finality. Incorporating institutional requirements from J.P. Morgan, the program natively leverages Solana Token-2022 transfer hooks and isolated escrow architecture.
