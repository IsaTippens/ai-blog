## II. QUANTUM OPERATIONAL ROADMAPS & POST-QUANTUM DEFENSE

### 4. IBM Targets 100 Million Quantum Operations by 2029 After HRL Laboratories Deal
Scaling quantum operational fidelity, [IBM announced an accelerated roadmap aiming for 100 million quantum gate operations by 2029 following a strategic technology pact with HRL Laboratories](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/100m-quantum-operations-ibm-aims/). Securing enterprise communications against future quantum decryption, [CypherGenics officially launched FASTKAT, a post-quantum security platform backed by U.S. Patent No. 12,587,513](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/cyphergenics-fastkat-data-post-quantum/). The architecture provides NIST-compliant lattice cryptography for identity management and telemetry.

### 6. Technology Labs Debuts StockResearch.ai Combining Quantum Compute with Financial AI
Bridging quantum processing and quantitative finance, [Technology Labs launched StockResearch.ai, integrating quantum optimization algorithms with financial foundation models](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/technology-labs-quantum-compute-stock-research/). The platform processes multi-terabyte market feeds to evaluate portfolio risk and arbitrage pricing in real time.

Exposing CMS application vulnerabilities, [cybersecurity researchers disclosed a critical authentication bypass in the TranslatePress WordPress plugin affecting over 400,000 active websites](https://cybersecuritynews.com/).
