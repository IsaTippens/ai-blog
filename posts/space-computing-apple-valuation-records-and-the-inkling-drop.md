## I. ORBITAL INFRASTRUCTURE & IN-SPACE COMPUTE

### 1. China Successfully Launches First Batch of Space-Computing Satellites Into Orbit
Highlighting a monumental paradigm shift in decentralized computing networks, [Shanghai Xingshu Tiansuan Space Technology Co. has successfully deployed the first batch of space-computing satellites](https://qna.org.qa/en/News-Area/News/2026-7/19/china-sends-first-space-computing-satellites-into-orbit). Part of a planned 1,000-satellite orbital computing constellation, these systems are engineered to process remote-sensing data, perform AI diagnostics, and run complex mathematical models directly in Earth's orbit rather than transmitting massive, bandwidth-heavy raw datasets back to terrestrial communication grids. This orbital-first compute model drastically reduces latency and alleviates data bottlenecks on ground stations, marking a practical breakthrough for sovereign, space-based computing frameworks.

## II. PUBLIC MARKETS & COGNITIVE HEGEMONY

### 2. Apple Reclaims Spot as the World's Most Valuable Company, Approaching $5 Trillion
In a striking, symbolic inversion of the dominant AI market narrative, [Apple has overtaken Nvidia to reclaim the crown as the world's most valuable company, approaching a $5 trillion valuation](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-18-2026). While Nvidia dominated the early stages of the AI gold rush by selling physical processors, public markets are now pivotally shifting rewards toward consumer-facing platforms that put AI models directly in front of billions of daily users. The milestone caps a historic stretch for Cupertino, which recently secured Apple Intelligence approvals in China via Alibaba, accelerated in-house custom server silicon developments, and expanded on-device model integrations.

## III. FRONTIER MODELS & CAPITAL CONSOLIDATION

### 3. Thinking Machines Releases "Inkling"—A 975B Open Model on a $2 Billion Seed Round
In one of the largest capital allocations in venture history, [Thinking Machines—founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati—has secured an unprecedented $2 billion seed funding round](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-18-2026). Simultaneously, the frontier AI developer has dropped **Inkling**, a massive [975-billion-parameter open-weight model](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-18-2026). Designed for high-throughput, localized, and multi-step logical reasoning, Inkling's release intensifies the market-wide shift from closed-source APIs to localized, open-weights deployment for enterprise software.

### 4. DeepSeek Prepares for Shanghai Listing at a Giant $74 Billion Valuation
Further shaking the competitive model landscape, high-disruption Chinese developer [DeepSeek is reportedly seeking new funding at a $74 billion valuation](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-18-2026). Preparing for a Shanghai listing next year, DeepSeek’s aggressive token pricing strategy (slashing costs roughly 75% below Western rivals) continues to drive substantial deflationary pricing pressure across the entire model industry, prompting major structural recalibrations in competitor monetization loops.

### 5. Fireworks AI Raises $1.5 Billion to Fuel Specialized Inference Architectures
Securing the capital necessary to run complex foundational models cheaply and at machine-scale velocity, developer infrastructure provider [Fireworks AI has raised $1.5 billion at a $17.5 billion valuation](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-18-2026). The capital is earmarked to rapidly expand Fireworks' high-speed inference frameworks, allowing enterprise clients to optimize runtime token distribution across massive agent networks.

### 6. Anthropic in Preliminary Talks with Meta for a $10 Billion Compute Lease
Demonstrating deep structural consolidation among top-tier AI developers, [Anthropic is reportedly in preliminary discussions to lease AI computing power from Meta in a deal valued at $10 billion](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-18-2026). If finalized, the transaction will allow Anthropic to utilize Meta’s massive, clean-energy-backed data center capacity to train and evaluate its upcoming next-generation Claude model family.
