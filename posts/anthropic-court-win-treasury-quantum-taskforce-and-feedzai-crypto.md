## II. SOVEREIGN QUANTUM DEFENSE & CRYOGENIC ARCHITECTURE

### 4. U.S. Treasury Department Launches Financial Quantum Task Force
Securing the nation's financial backbone against Q-Day threats, [Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent officially launched the Quantum-Readiness Task Force to mandate post-quantum cryptography (PQC) across Tier-1 banks and clearing houses](https://aktualita.co/en/international/us-forms-quantum-task-force-to-protect-financial-systems/). The initiative establishes strict timetables for migrating core banking ledgers to NIST-standardized algorithms (ML-KEM and ML-DSA).

### 5. Post-Quantum Encryption Standards Expand Across Digital Identity Networks
Defending citizen verification systems, [telecom and biometric infrastructure providers are deploying post-quantum cryptographic handshakes across national eID programs and digital identity gates](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/post-quantum-security-spreads-across-digital-identity-infrastructure/). The transition protects long-lived biometric identity templates against "harvest now, decrypt later" adversary intelligence operations.

### 6. SEEQC Details On-Chip Cryogenic Digital Logic to Eliminate Dilution Wiring Bottlenecks
Overcoming hardware scaling barriers, [quantum architecture pioneer SEEQC published research demonstrating how Single Flux Quantum (SFQ) digital logic operating at cryogenic temperatures eliminates thousands of coaxial cables](https://thequbitreport.com/). The on-chip architecture provides the multiplexing density required to scale multi-chip quantum processors past 10,000 physical qubits.
