### 1. Google Quantum AI Lowers Qubit Threshold to Break Bitcoin Cryptography
In a landmark cryptanalytic study, [Google Quantum AI researchers revealed that breaking the secp256k1 elliptic curve curve used in Bitcoin requires as few as 1,200 logical qubits and 90 million Toffoli gates](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/google-quantum-attack-bitcoin-estimates-closer/). Validated using zero-knowledge proofs, the mathematical findings demonstrate that emerging fast-clock quantum systems could intercept and decrypt unconfirmed cryptocurrency transactions directly within public mempools.

### 2. Quantum Error Correction Accelerated 42x via qLDPC Codes on Neutral Atoms
Overcoming fault-tolerant decoding bottlenecks, [physicists demonstrated a 42x speedup in quantum error correction cycle times by deploying quantum low-density parity-check (qLDPC) codes on neutral-atom processors](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/quantum-error-correction-qldpc-codes-speedup-neutral-atoms/). The architecture drastically reduces physical-to-logical qubit overhead for quantum simulation.
