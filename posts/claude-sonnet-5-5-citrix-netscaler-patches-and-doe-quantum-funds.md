## II. PERIMETER CYBERSECURITY & EMERGENCY ZERO-DAY PATCHES

### 4\. Citrix Patches NetScaler RCE Zero-Days Exploited for Weeks (CVE-2026-88771 & CVE-2026-88772)

Resolving a critical perimeter defense crisis, [Citrix released emergency patches for two actively exploited remote code execution flaws in NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/28/citrix-netscaler-rce-zero-days-exploited-for-weeks-cve-2026-88771-cve-2026-88772/). Designated CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772, the vulnerabilities permit unauthenticated remote attackers to execute arbitrary system code on enterprise boundary appliances. Forensic investigations by [watchTowr](https://watchtowr.com/intelligence/citrix-netscaler-adc-citrix-netscaler-gateway-remote-code-execution-cve-2026-88771/) and [Sophos](https://www.sophos.com/en-us/blog/citrix-netscaler-cve-2026-88771-cve-2026-88772-in-active-exploitation) confirmed adversaries had been leveraging memory corruption primitives in wild attacks prior to public disclosure.

### 5\. CISA Amplifies Emergency Directives for NetScaler Perimeter Remediation

Enforcing federal vulnerability management protocols, [CISA issued an amplified security alert regarding the Citrix NetScaler vulnerabilities](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/27/critical-zero-day-vulnerabilities-exploited-citrix-netscaler-adc-gateway). Security operations centers across government and critical infrastructure sectors were directed to verify gateway firmware integrity, review authentication logs for abnormal reverse-proxy sessions, and apply patched builds immediately.
