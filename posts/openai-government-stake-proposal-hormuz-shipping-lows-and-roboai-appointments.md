## I. COGNITIVE ALIGNMENT & STATE-LEVEL PROPOSALS

### 1. OpenAI Proposes 5% U.S. Government Equity Stake to Address Security and Antitrust Concerns
In an unprecedented corporate move to navigate intense national security, antitrust, and ethical alignment reviews, [OpenAI has formally floated a plan to grant the U.S. Government a 5% equity stake](https://quasa.io/media/openai-s-5-us-government-stake-proposal-examined). Citing the company's preparations for a historic $730 billion initial public offering (IPO), the proposal is designed to give the federal government a direct stake in the lab's governance, audit trails, and future profitability. If approved, the equity transfer would represent a landmark corporate-state partnership, giving state nodes unprecedented access to frontier model architectures.

### 2. Former INTERPOL President Ahmed Naser Al-Raisi Joins Robo.ai to Lead Sovereign AI
Shaping the deeptech landscape in the Middle East, Robo.ai Inc. (NASDAQ: AIIO) has appointed [H.E. Major General Dr. Ahmed Naser Al-Raisi, the former President of INTERPOL, as the Chairman of its subsidiary Neurovia AI](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260713cn02734/former-interpol-president-he-ahmed-naser-al-raisi-joins-roboai-as-chairman-of-neurovia-ai-to-build-trusted-sovereign-ai-infrastructure-in-the-uae-and-gcc-region). Dr. Al-Raisi will lead Neurovia’s strategy to construct trusted, highly secure, and sovereign AI computing infrastructures and high-throughput data centers across the UAE and the broader GCC region, shoring up regional compute independence.

### 7. India's CERT-In Outlines New Regulatory Frameworks for AI-Driven Cybersecurity

### 8. Approximately 35% of Bitcoin's Circulating Supply Vulnerable to Quantum Attack
Illustrating the urgent need for post-quantum ledger transition, an [unpublished June 2026 working paper indicates that roughly 35% of Bitcoin's circulating supply](https://finance.biggo.com/news/4fa04535-a4ad-43f9-98fa-16f09ae73119) remains vulnerable to a quantum computer ECDSA attack. The exposure belongs to legacy, unspent transaction output (UTXO) addresses that expose public keys, highlighting the critical priority of post-quantum cryptographic (PQC) ledgers.
