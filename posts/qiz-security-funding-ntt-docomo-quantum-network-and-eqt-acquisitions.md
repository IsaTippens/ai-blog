## I. SOVEREIGN QUANTUM BOUNDARIES & NETWORKS

### 1. QIZ Security Closes $17 Million Seed Funding Round for Post-Quantum Platforms
As enterprises face accelerating timelines for post-quantum cryptographic (PQC) migration, security developer [QIZ Security has closed a $17 million seed funding round](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/qiz-security-post-quantum-cybersecurity-17m/). The capital is earmarked to rapidly scale the deployment of QIZ's enterprise-grade cryptographic agility and migration management platforms, capturing early market share as regulatory compliance frameworks begin enforcing post-quantum resilience globally.

### 2. NTT Docomo Links Tokyo, Nagoya, and Osaka via 600km Quantum QKD Network
In a major milestone for physical quantum communications, [NTT Docomo Business has successfully deployed a massive 600km quantum network](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/qiz-security-post-quantum-cybersecurity-17m/) connecting Tokyo, Nagoya, and Osaka. The long-distance network utilizes advanced **Quantum Key Distribution (QKD)** technology over standard fiber links to safeguard critical telecommunications, grid operations, and cloud data center interfaces against potential future decryption threats.

### 3. Google Quantum Researchers Cut Surface Code Error Rate via Reinforcement Learning
Sustaining rapid hardware progress, [Google's quantum engineering team has published a breakthrough study](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/qiz-security-post-quantum-cybersecurity-17m/) detailing a significant reduction in surface code quantum error rates to **7.72 × 10^-4**. The team leveraged deep reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms to optimize real-time qubit state control and error-correcting cycles, marking a vital step forward in the race to build stable, fault-tolerant quantum processors.

## II. THE AI DATA CENTER GRID & CAPITAL MONETIZATION

### 4. EQT to Acquire Copia Power to Build Integrated AI Infrastructure Platform
Shifting the clean power and computing landscape, investment firm [EQT has signed a definitive agreement to acquire Copia Power](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260710hs01714/eqt-to-acquire-copia-power-a-leading-integrated-power-and-ai-infrastructure-platform). Copia Power is a leading developer of integrated clean power and digital infrastructure, and the acquisition is designed to secure high-capacity renewable energy grids to support Meta, Google, and Microsoft's multi-billion dollar, high-density AI data center campuses.

### 5. Slate's proposed Hamilton AI Data Center Campus Sparks Massive Protests
Illustrating intense community pushback against data center resource consumption, [Hamilton Mayor Andrea Horwath has called a special council meeting](https://thepublicrecord.ca/2026/07/horwath-calls-special-council-meeting-to-hear-from-ai-data-centre-proponents-ahead-of-bylaw-vote/) for July 14, 2026. The meeting will address Slate Asset Management's severance application for a massive data center campus on former Stelco lands, known as Steelport, which has drawn a record-breaking **1,209 letters of opposition** from local residents worried about energy consumption, water depletion, and noise pollution.

### 6. California Senate Committee Approves Landmark AB 412 Copyright Bill
Enforcing transparency across foundational model training sets, California's landmark [AB 412 copyright transparency and AI audit bill has won full Senate Appropriations Committee approval](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/ai-legislative-update-july10-2026).
