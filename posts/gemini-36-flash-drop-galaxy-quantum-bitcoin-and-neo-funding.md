## I. FRONTIER MODEL DROPS & EFFICIENCY ARCHITECTURE

### 1. Google Releases Gemini 3.6 Flash and Security-Tuned 3.5 Flash Cyber

Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber is specifically tuned for real-time automated vulnerability detection and threat telemetry analysis, allowing SOC teams to parse millions of network events in real time.

### 2. Leaks Detail Google's "Frozen v2" Custom Silicon 10x Efficiency Boost
Hardware sources confirm that [Google is engineering a new server chip codenamed Frozen v2](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-21-2026).
