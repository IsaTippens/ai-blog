## I. HYPERSCALE CAPEX & FRONTIER POLICY

### 1. Big Tech Amasses $1.09 Trillion in Unstarted Data Center Lease Liabilities
Illustrating the staggering financial commitments driving the artificial intelligence boom, [SEC filings reveal that Microsoft, Meta, Oracle, Amazon, and Alphabet have accumulated $1.09 trillion in unstarted lease commitments](https://virginiabusiness.com/big-tech-commits-1-trillion-ai-data-centre-leases/). These long-term liabilities represent future payments for third-party hyperscale data centers currently under construction, underlining intense competition to secure high-density power grids and server space.

### 2. White House Exempts Open-Weight AI Systems from Government Security Vetting
In a major policy decision presented during closed-door briefings with tech executives, [the White House confirmed that open-weight AI models will be exempted from government security reviews](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/08/04/white-house-will-exempt-open-ai-systems-security-review/). Developed under President Trump's June Executive Order, the voluntary pre-release vetting framework will concentrate federal scrutiny exclusively on closed proprietary systems from frontier laboratories like OpenAI and Anthropic.

### 3. Goldman Sachs Projects $1 Trillion Global AI Investment Pace
Highlighting macroeconomic capital flows, [Goldman Sachs released a global economic outlook projecting total annual AI investment to hit $1 trillion by the end of 2026](https://iafrica.com/goldman-sachs-puts-2026-global-ai-investment-at-1-trillion-which-makes-africas-share-smaller-than-commonly-cited/). The bank noted that while hardware infrastructure dominates capex, enterprise software integration is accelerating across Tier-1 financial hubs.

## II. NEXT-GEN SEMICONDUCTORS & STORAGE ARCHITECTURE

### 4. Samsung Unveils V10 400+ Layer BV-NAND and zHBM Concept at FMS 2026
Opening the Future of Memory and Storage (FMS 2026) conference in Santa Clara, [Samsung Electronics showcased its next-generation 3D-Memory roadmap, featuring industry-first 400-plus layer V10 BV-NAND](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-unveils-next-gen-3d-memory-vision-at-fms-2026-charting-the-future-of-ai-infrastructure/). Samsung also previewed zHBM and zNAND-O concept models specifically engineered to eliminate bandwidth bottlenecks in ultra-large AI training clusters.

### 5. FCC Proposes Rules to Block Chinese AI Hardware in US Networks
Addressing hardware supply chain security, [the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) introduced proposed rulemakings aimed at blocking Chinese AI hardware and specialized accelerators](https://news.northeastern.edu/2026/08/04/us-china-ai-competition/) from being deployed in U.S. telecommunications and cloud computing infrastructure.
