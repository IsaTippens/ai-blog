### 1\. BTQ Technologies and QPerfect Deliver First Circuit-Level Implementation of Quantum One-Shot Signatures

Establishing a physical foundation for unforgeable digital tokens and trustless delegation without distributed ledger overhead, [BTQ Technologies and its French subsidiary QPerfect published the first explicit circuit-level implementation of a quantum one-shot signature (OSS) scheme](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/btq-technologies-and-qperfect-deliver-first-circuit-level-implementation-of-quantum-one-shot-signatures/). Detailed in the preprint *A quantum algorithm for one-shot signatures*, the architecture translates abstract "local quantum cryptography" into concrete quantum gate instructions. By encoding private signing keys into fragile quantum superposition states stored in local quantum memory, the primitive enforces single-use cryptographic security directly through measurement collapse and the quantum no-cloning theorem. Once a signature is executed, the underlying quantum state irreversibly collapses, physically preventing a secret key from signing a second message and eliminating the need for global blockchain consensus to prevent double-spending.

Earlier theoretical proposals for one-shot signatures depended on complex permutable pseudorandom permutations (PPRPs) that lacked tractable circuit syntheses. BTQ and QPerfect resolved this bottleneck by replacing the PPRP layer with a single puncturable pseudorandom function (PPRF) built on a Goldreich-Goldwasser-Micali (GGM) tree lookup coupled with a reversible Bruhat decomposition subroutine to sample uniform superpositions over random affine cosets (`A_y * z + b_y`). To sign arbitrary `l`\-bit messages without applying iterative bit-by-bit phase-flipping gates, the circuit executes a global measurement routine paired with a single dual-phase translation oracle (`D_{p->m}`). The team validated the complete gate architecture on QPerfect's MIMIQ emulator—which was also [integrated into the qBraid cloud platform today](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/btq-technologies-integrates-qperfect-mimiq-quantum-emulator-into-qbraid-cloud-platform/)—confirming a logical footprint of `Theta(kappa * log(r) + n + l)` qubits and `Theta(n^3 + n * l)` gate complexity, scaling from 206 to 2,438 logical qubits across security parameters `lambda = 2` to `10`.

### 2\. Sandia National Laboratories and Quantinuum Map Fault-Tolerant Logical Qubit Noise via Logical GST

Advancing the precision characterization of fault-tolerant quantum processors, [Sandia National Laboratories and Quantinuum introduced "Logical Gate Set Tomography" (Logical GST) on the 20-qubit trapped-ion Quantinuum System Model H1-1](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/sandia-and-quantinuum-introduce-logical-gate-set-tomography-to-map-fault-tolerant-qubit-noise/) ([GQI Technical Diagram](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/wp-content/themes/qcr-newsroom/assets/images/gqi-logo-official.png)). Executed as the final experimental campaign on the H1-1 apparatus, the study achieved the first self-consistent, full tomographic reconstruction of a universal single-qubit logical gate set encoded within the `[[7, 1, 3]]` Steane code using seven data qubits and six syndrome degrees of freedom. Unlike conventional logical benchmarking that relies on software-based Pauli-frame tracking—which artificially twirls and masks coherent phase errors—the research team executed active quantum error correction (QEC) via real-time classical feedforward, applying physical correction pulses directly to the trapped ions inside the gate zones.

Standard fault-tolerant threshold theorems assume that QEC syndrome extraction converts complex physical errors into uncorrelated Pauli-stochastic noise channels. While the 50-shot experimental H1-1 dataset across 158 compressed circuits aligned with a Pauli-stochastic profile, high-resolution 14,100-shot simulations on Quantinuum's H1-1E emulator uncovered statistically significant logical non-Markovianity (`N_sigma_CPTP` \~ 4.9) and coherent logical over-rotations (`Delta_theta` \~ 0.0015 pi on `SL`, `IL`, and `TL`). By selectively toggling physical noise channels in the emulator, the team proved that physical dephasing accumulated during ion transport and sympathetic cooling generates coherent logical drift, reinforcing [Quantinuum's engineering thesis that raw physical two-qubit gate fidelity trumps raw clock speed in the fault-tolerant scaling race](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantinuum-chief-scientist-argues-physical-fidelity-trumps-gate-speed-in-fault-tolerant-scaling-race/).

* **GitLab CE/EE Integer Overflow RCE (`CVE-2026-97229`, CVSS 10.0)**: A maximum-severity integer-overflow and wraparound defect in GitLab Community and Enterprise Editions (affecting versions 19.2 prior to 19.2.8, 19.3 prior to 19.3.5, and 19.4 prior to 19.4.2) that permits unauthenticated remote attackers to execute arbitrary code via crafted regular expressions in CI/CD pipeline configurations, with Alpine Linux disclosing a near-miss intrusion attempt against its self-hosted GitLab infrastructure.
