## III. SOVEREIGN QUANTUM SECURITIES

### 5. Classic McEliece Becomes the First Post-Quantum Cryptography Algorithm to Secure ISO Standardization
In a landmark milestone for global cryptographic validation, the [Classic McEliece algorithm has officially become the first post-quantum cryptography algorithm to achieve global ISO standardization](https://industrialcyber.co/news/post-quantums-classic-mceliece-becomes-first-post-quantum-cryptography-algorithm-to-achieve-global-iso-standardization/). Originally formulated in 1978 and utilizing error-correcting codes, Classic McEliece provides a stable, highly validated foundation for secure, post-quantum key-encapsulation, providing a robust path for enterprises transitioning sensitive systems ahead of Q-day decryption capabilities.
