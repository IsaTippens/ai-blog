## I. CYBERSECURITY, FINTECH & BANKING MANDATES

### 1. ECB Orders European Banks to Submit Board-Approved "AI Defence Plans" Citing DORA
In a direct response to the weaponization of generative models, the [European Central Bank (ECB) has issued a formal letter to significant institutions](https://financialregulation.linklaters.com/post/102n8h1/ecb-tells-eu-banks-to-submit-ai-defence-plans). The central bank warns that emerging cognitive platforms can identify software, endpoint, and architectural vulnerabilities at unprecedented speed, drastically lowering the barrier for systemic exploits. Under the strict requirements of the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA), the ECB is forcing banks to assess the threat landscape, outline immediate measures for vulnerability and patch management, secure externally exposed assets, and manage third-party software risks in formal, board-approved action plans.

### 2. FinTech Junction 2026 Convenes in Tel Aviv
As transactions increasingly migrate to autonomous frameworks, the annual [FinTech Junction 2026 summit has officially opened in Tel Aviv](https://www.tradingview.com/news/financewire:f8a9b3f38094b:0-fintech-junction-2026-returns-to-tel-aviv-on-july-8-with-israel-s-payments-ai-and-cybersecurity-leaders/). High-profile discussions among payments, AI, and cybersecurity leaders are focusing on the security of multi-party computation (MPC) digital wallets, decentralized real-time clearing houses, and the deployment of runtime guardrails to prevent AI agent transactions from going out of bounds.

### 3. European Systems Suffer Advanced AI-Assisted Cyberattacks
Reinforcing the urgency of the ECB's warning, European security agencies report that an advanced cyberattack targeting critical European system networks was [highly likely executed with the assistance of real-time AI tools](https://www.ruv.is/english/2026-07-08-cyberattack-likely-assisted-by-ai-480804). The attack utilized automated script generation and real-time vulnerability probing to navigate security barriers, highlighting the transition of cyber warfare from manual scripting to machine-scale velocity.

### 7. Google Rolls Out Final Pixel 6 Security Update, Consolidating Silicon Support
Marking the definitive sunset of a landmark device generation, Google has officially rolled out its [July 2026 security patch for the Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro](https://tech-ish.com/2026/07/08/pixel-6-july-2026-security-update/). The release represents the end of the five-year support lifecycle for Google's first-generation in-house Tensor silicon, as the hardware giant consolidates its software, update, and patch pipelines around newer generations of G-series custom chips.
