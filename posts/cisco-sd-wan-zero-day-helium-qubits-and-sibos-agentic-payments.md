## II. CRYOGENIC QUANTUM MATERIALS & COHERENCE SCALING

### 4\. University of Surrey Proposes Superfluid Helium Qubit with 100x Error Reduction

Overcoming dielectric loss and charge noise in solid-state quantum processors, [physicists at the University of Surrey designed a novel qubit using electrons trapped on superfluid helium](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260930020309.htm). By floating individual electrons above an atomically pristine liquid helium-4 surface, [the architecture shields delicate quantum information from electromagnetic interference](https://news.ssbcrack.com/new-superfluid-helium-qubit-could-revolutionize-quantum-computing-by-reducing-error-rates/), projecting a 100-fold reduction in physical gate error rates compared to planar superconducting transmon designs.

### 5\. Institute for Quantum Computing Inks Cross-Border Italian Strategic Partnership

Expanding European quantum communications infrastructure, [the Institute for Quantum Computing (IQC) at Waterloo established a strategic partnership with Italian quantum research institutions](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/perimeter-institute-quantum-computing-lutkenhaus-leads/). Led by Norbert Lütkenhaus, the initiative focuses on deploying post-quantum cryptographic key exchange protocols and quantum repeater networks across Mediterranean telecom testbeds.
