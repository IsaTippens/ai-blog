## I. AGENTIC FINTECH & SOVEREIGN CAPITAL

### 1. CaixaBank Completes First Real-World AI Agentic Payment with Visa
Marking a landmark milestone in autonomous commerce, Spain's [CaixaBank has successfully completed the first real-world payment](https://www.fintechfutures.com/ai-in-fintech/caixabank-ai-agent-payment-visa) initiated autonomously by an AI agent acting on behalf of a human cardholder. The transaction utilized the "Intelligent Commerce" framework and "Agentic Ready" program developed by project partner Visa. The bank said the pilot used real card data on standard merchant systems secured by Visa's existing tokenization, ID verification, and fraud monitoring systems, proving the absolute technical feasibility of agent-initiated transactions within current infrastructure. Competing payment network Mastercard has also claimed its own first agentic transaction last week in France, working with Crédit Agricole and Worldline.

### 2. Kling AI Closes $3B Funding Round at $18B Valuation
Demonstrating the extreme scale of AI capital consolidation in Asia, Chinese developer [Kling AI has closed a massive $3 billion funding round](https://techfundingnews.com/worldmodeldata-7m-seed-ai-training-data-video-games/) at an **$18 billion valuation**. Backed heavily by tech giants Tencent and Alibaba, the funding is designed to scale Kling's foundation video-generation models, directly rivaling OpenAI's Sora platform on the global stage.

### 3. UK VC Growth in H1 2026 Driven Entirely by AI
Venture capital and deeptech funding reports reveal that UK startups raised **$17 billion** during the first half of 2026, as noted by [TechFundingNews](https://techfundingnews.com/worldmodeldata-7m-seed-ai-training-data-video-games/). Crucially, a massive **74%** of that total VC capital was allocated directly to artificial intelligence startups, nearly doubling AI's venture market dominance from the previous year.

### 4. Worldmodeldata Raises £7M Seed with Ex-Meta Policy VP joining Board
In early-stage deeptech, synthetic AI training data provider [Worldmodeldata has raised £7 million in seed funding](https://techfundingnews.com/worldmodeldata-7m-seed-ai-training-data-video-games/) to scale its video game synthetic training data and physical simulation library past 1 million hours of data. To navigate complex IP, fair use, and licensing rules, a former Meta Policy VP is joining their board to guide their scaling strategy.

## II. INDUSTRIAL TECH CONSOLIDATION & HARDWARE

### 5. Schneider Electric to Acquire Industrial AI Software Cognite for $3.1 Billion
In a major corporate consolidation, [Schneider Electric has agreed to acquire Cognite for $3.1 billion](https://industrialcyber.co/news/avalue-technology-expands-torizon-integration-to-help-oems-meet-cyber-resilience-act-requirements/). The strategic acquisition is engineered to expand Schneider's industrial AI and operational data capabilities, integrating Cognite's flagship "Cognite Data Fusion" platform into Schneider’s industrial software suite to scale AI-driven operations for global manufacturing and energy infrastructure clients.

## III. CYBERSECURITY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

### 7. TXOne Launches Virtual Portable Inspector for Distributed OT Sites
OT security vendor [TXOne has launched its Virtual Portable Inspector](https://industrialcyber.co/news/avalue-technology-expands-torizon-integration-to-help-oems-meet-cyber-resilience-act-requirements/) platform. The tool is engineered to scale automated asset inspections, vulnerability detection, and malware scans across distributed industrial sites and manufacturing networks without physical device attachments.

### 8. Avalue Technology Integrates Torizon to Help OEMs Meet EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)
To help original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) prepare for the European Union's upcoming **Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)**, industrial computing vendor [Avalue Technology has expanded its integration with Toradex's "Torizon" platform](https://industrialcyber.co/news/avalue-technology-expands-torizon-integration-to-help-oems-meet-cyber-resilience-act-requirements/). The integration provides built-in secure over-the-air (OTA) updates, software bills of materials (SBOM) generation, and cryptographic device identity to protect hardware endpoints.

### 9. Five Eyes Agencies Warn of Surge in AI-Accelerated Cyber Threats
Five Eyes cybersecurity and intelligence agencies have released a joint warning, detailed by the [Australian Industry Group](https://www.australianindustrygroup.com.au/news/blogs/2026/how-ai-is-accelerating-cyber-threats-even-quicker-than-you-think/), warning that hostile nation-states and criminal cybercartels are deploying real-time generative AI to accelerate social engineering, automate malware mutations, and scan critical infrastructures for zero-day vulnerabilities.
