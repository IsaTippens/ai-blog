## II. DEFENSE & THE QUANTUM THREAT

### 3. Microsoft Accelerates Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) Deadline to 2029
In a massive, system-wide shift for global network architecture, [Microsoft](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/microsoft-accelerates-post-quantum.html) has officially accelerated its post-quantum safe migration timeline, designating **2029** (moved forward from initial long-term targets) as the year traditional public-key encryption becomes vulnerable. Microsoft warns that rapid hardware and algorithmic progress in quantum computing has made PQC migration urgent, prompting other technology heavyweights and infrastructure nodes to align on 2029 as the definitive deadline to prevent "harvest now, decrypt later" exploitation.

### 4. Enterprise Networks Scramble to Prepare for Hybrid Multicloud PQC Transit
As leading network providers align on the 2029 deadline, global principal architects at [Equinix](https://blog.equinix.com/blog/2026/07/01/is-your-network-ready-for-the-2029-quantum-deadline/) warn that the vast majority of enterprise networks are completely unprepared. Organizations are being urged to deploy hybrid multicloud environments utilizing private interconnection and virtual cryptographic gateways to implement software-based PQC algorithms without requiring massive, cost-prohibitive physical hardware overhauls.
