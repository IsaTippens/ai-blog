## I. AGENTIC ENTERPRISE & AUTOMATED INSURTECH

### 1. NTT DATA Launches 'AI for Insurance' Agentic Platform
Automating core carrier workflows, [NTT DATA officially introduced 'NTT DATA AI for Insurance'](https://www.nttdata.com/global/en/news/press-release/2026/august/080601). Built on its proprietary AIVista platform, the Service-as-Software solution combines configurable AI agents, domain-specific data models, and enterprise governance controls to automate complex underwriting, claims processing, and policy administration across regulated carriers.

### 2. Obsidian Security Raises $85 Million to Police Rogue AI Agents
Shoring up SaaS security posture, [Obsidian Security secured $85 million in new funding](https://fintech.global/2026/08/05/whos-managing-your-ai-agents-in-tax-due-diligence/) to deploy governance platforms that audit and contain autonomous AI agents. The software detects prompt injections, unauthorized privilege escalation, and un-vetted API actions executed by autonomous software agents inside enterprise networks.

### 3. Sumsub and Sumvin Unveil Identity Framework for AI Agent Transactions
Addressing autonomous commerce, [Sumsub and Sumvin launched a specialized identity and anti-fraud framework enabling AI agents to execute financial transactions](https://fintech.global/2026/08/05/whos-managing-your-ai-agents-in-tax-due-diligence/). The platform binds biometric verification from human operators to agentic wallets, setting cryptographic spend limits and transaction policies.

### 4. London Regulators Clear Self-Driving Robotaxis for Street Deployment

The decision follows extensive safety trials and establishes operational guidelines for commercial autonomous fleets in dense metropolitan corridors.

### 5. City Colleges of Chicago Launches First Associate AI Degree Program
Expanding community technology pipelines, [City Colleges of Chicago (CCC) announced its first dedicated Associate in Applied Science (AAS) degree in Artificial Intelligence](https://colleges.ccc.edu/2026/08/05/city-colleges-of-chicago-launches-its-first-ai-degree-program/). Developed alongside industry partners, the curriculum equips students with practical skills in model fine-tuning, prompt engineering, data pipeline maintenance, and AI safety protocols.
