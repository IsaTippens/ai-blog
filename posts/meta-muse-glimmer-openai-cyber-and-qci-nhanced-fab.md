## I. FRONTIER MODEL WEIGHTS & OPEN-SOURCE STRATEGY

### 1. Meta Open-Sources Muse Glimmer and Announces Muse Spark 1.2 Weights
Challenging closed foundation model labs, [Meta open-sourced Muse Glimmer, a compact agentic AI model designed to run on consumer-grade single GPU workstations](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-august-11-2026). CEO Mark Zuckerberg also confirmed plans to open-source the larger Muse Spark 1.2 weights, urging US regulators to remove barriers to open-source development to compete with Chinese foundation labs.

### 2. OpenAI Unveils Purpose-Trained GPT-5.6-Cyber via Daybreak Red
Strengthening defensive cybersecurity infrastructure, [OpenAI launched GPT-5.6-Cyber, a specialized model purposed for vulnerability identification and security research](https://www.dawan.africa/news/openai-unveils-new-model-to-help-defenders-detect-software-vulnerabilities-in-systems). The model is available exclusively to approved defenders and red teams via OpenAI's new "Daybreak Red" authorization tier.

## II. QUANTUM HARDWARE MANUFACTURING & GRID OPTIMIZATION

### 3. QCi Reports Q2 Financials and Acquires NHanced Semiconductors to Launch Fab 2
Expanding domestic quantum hardware fabrication, [Quantum Computing Inc. (QCi) reported Q2 revenue of $5.6 million and announced the strategic acquisition of NHanced Semiconductors](https://quantumcomputinginc.com/news/press-releases/2026/quantum-computing-inc.-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results). The acquisition launches Fab 2, providing QCi with in-house 3D semiconductor packaging and volume prototyping for room-temperature photonic QPUs.

### 4. Infleqtion Selected by Eaton for Air Force Quantum Grid Optimization Program
Applying neutral-atom quantum processors to utility infrastructure, [Infleqtion received an Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) subcontract from Eaton to evaluate quantum computing for electrical grid contingency analysis](https://infleqtion.com/infleqtion-selected-by-eaton-to-support-research-advancing-quantum-computing-for-u-s-grid-resilience/). The multi-year program uses neutral-atom QPUs to optimize large-scale grid routing and prevent cascading power outages.

## III. CYBERSECURITY THREAT TELEMETRY & ADVISORIES

### 5. US and South Korea Issue Joint Advisory on GUNRA Ransomware Group
Targeting cross-border cybercrime, [the FBI, CISA, NSA, and South Korea's National Office of Investigation issued a joint security advisory targeting the GUNRA ransomware cartel](https://en.sedaily.com/society/2026/08/11/korea-us-team-up-against-gunra-ransomware-gang-targeting). The group exploits unpatched VPN gateways to exfiltrate enterprise data from manufacturing, healthcare, and financial institutions prior to encryption.

### 6. Indonesian Deputy Minister Warns of Quantum Cyber Threats and "Harvest Now" Exploits
Emphasizing post-quantum urgency, [Indonesian Deputy Minister Nezar Patria launched the ITSEC Cyber & AI Academy, warning that threat actors are actively harvesting encrypted data for future quantum decryption](https://en.antaranews.com/news/426485/deputy-minister-nezar-patria-warns-of-quantum-cyber-threat). He urged critical infrastructure operators to accelerate migration toward NIST-compliant post-quantum cryptography (PQC).

### 7. TCS Responds to Dark Web Data Leak Claims Following Threat Intelligence Alert
Addressing data breach claims, [Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) informed stock exchanges that internal threat investigations found no evidence of system compromise](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/tcs-flags-data-leak-claims-finds-no-breach/articleshow/133139972.cms) following dark web forum listings alleging the sale of employee records.
