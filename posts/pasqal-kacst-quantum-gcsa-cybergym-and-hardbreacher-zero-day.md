## I. SOVEREIGN QUANTUM DEFENSE & HARDWARE ALLIANCES

Accelerating post-quantum silicon development, [semiconductor security firm SEALSQ announced a $24.5 million strategic investment vehicle targeting pure-play quantum computing startups and cryogenic ASIC designers](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/sealsq-investment-quantum-invests-245m/).
