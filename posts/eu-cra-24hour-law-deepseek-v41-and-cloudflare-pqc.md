## I. GLOBAL SOFTWARE LIABILITY & CYBER RESILIENCE REGULATIONS

### 1. EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) Mandatory 24-Hour Reporting Law Goes Live
Transforming global software supply chain compliance, [the European Union's Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation EU 2024/2847) officially took effect on September 11, activating mandatory 24-hour vulnerability reporting](https://www.threatlocker.com/blog/eu-cyber-resilience-act-24-hour-vulnerability-reporting-begins-sept-11). Manufacturers of hardware and software products with digital elements sold across European markets must now report actively exploited vulnerabilities directly to ENISA's Single Reporting Platform and designated Computer Security Incident Response Teams (CSIRTs) within 24 hours of detection, as analyzed by [Eversheds Sutherland](https://www.eversheds-sutherland.com/en/latvia/insights/cyber-resilience-act-september-2026) and [Taylor Wessing](https://www.taylorwessing.com/en/insights-and-events/insights/2026/09/cra-reporting-requirements).

### 2. Global Banking Regulators Close Enforcement Gaps on Outsourced Third-Party IT Risk
Tightening financial system operational resilience, [international financial regulators introduced coordinated frameworks to audit outsourced cloud and IT dependencies](https://fintech.global/2026/09/10/regulators-close-the-gap-on-outsourced-risk/). The guidelines mandate continuous automated auditing of critical software vendors and cross-border payment processors ahead of 2027 compliance cutoffs.

## III. POST-QUANTUM INTERNET HARDENING & LOGICAL QUBITS

### 6. Cloudflare Deploys Post-Quantum DNSSEC on 1.1.1.1 Public Resolver
In a milestone for public cryptographic infrastructure, [Cloudflare rolled out post-quantum DNSSEC across its 1.1.1.1 public DNS resolver network](https://blog.cloudflare.com/post-quantum-dnssec-1111/). By implementing NIST-standardized ML-DSA and Falcon signatures on 2,420-byte DNS packets, Cloudflare demonstrated that quantum-safe domain validation can operate across production internet traffic without exceeding packet fragmentation limits.

### 7. IQM Announces Initiative to Construct Europe's First Logical Qubit Quantum Computer
Pioneering fault-tolerant quantum processing, [European hardware manufacturer IQM Quantum Computers announced plans to build Europe's first quantum computer based on logical qubits](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/logical-qubits-iqm-europes-first/). The system deploys surface-code quantum error correction on superconducting processors, moving toward error-mitigated industrial simulation.

### 8. India's QNu Labs Raises ₹200 Crore ($24M) to Scale Post-Quantum Cyber Defense
Expanding sovereign post-quantum infrastructure, [Bengaluru-based deeptech developer QNu Labs secured ₹200 crore in funding](https://fintech.global/2026/09/10/qnu-labs-raises-%E2%82%B9200cr-to-scale-quantum-safe-cybersecurity/) to manufacture quantum key distribution (QKD) hardware and deploy post-quantum software algorithms across defense and telecommunications networks in South and Southeast Asia.
