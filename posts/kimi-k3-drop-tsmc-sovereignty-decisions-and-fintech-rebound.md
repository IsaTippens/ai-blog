### 1. Moonshot AI Launches Kimi K3 as the Largest Open-Weights Model Ever Released

Concurrently, Google is rolling out its long-awaited [Gemini 3.5 Pro model](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-17-2026) and officially re-branding NotebookLM to **Gemini Notebook**, fully integrating the research workspace into its primary ecosystem.

### 2. China Releases Strategic International AI Ethics Action Plan
Coordinating global rules around cognitive models, [China has officially released its International AI Ethics Action Plan](https://www.chinadailyasia.com/article/636647). Released on July 17, 2026, the comprehensive framework urges international alignment on safe, inclusive, and responsible model weights. The plan aligns with the establishment of the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO), which has just signed on [29 countries as founding members](https://www.nation.com.pk/18-Jul-2026/29-countries-sign-agreement-establish-world-ai-cooperation-organisation), including [Indonesia](https://en.antaranews.com/news/423160/indonesia-joins-waico-as-founding-member-to-boost-ai-development) to foster collaborative digital trade.

### 3. Vietnam AI Innovation Challenge 2026 Kicks Off 48-Hour Hackathon
Fostering regional AI-native software talent, the final round of the [Vietnam AI Innovation Challenge 2026 has officially opened in Hanoi](https://vietnam.vnanet.vn/english/tin-tuc/innovation-challenge-seeks-ai-native-solutions-to-real-world-problems-457398.html). Organized by the National Innovation Centre (NIC), Meta, NVIDIA, and Google Research, the 48-hour hackathon tasks over 300 international finalist teams with engineering AI-native solutions to complex real-world utility and public-sector problems. Concurrently, the committee announced the launch of the International Artificial Intelligence Olympiad 2027 (IAIO 2027), scheduled to take place in Vietnam.

## II. SEMICONDUCTOR SOVEREIGNTY & GEOPOLITICAL LINES

### 4. Taiwan to Keep Its Most Advanced Semiconductor Technologies Domestic
Even as TSMC finalized a landmark $265 billion agreement to build out sub-2nm factories in the United States, [Taiwan's Ministry of Economic Affairs has declared that the country will retain its most advanced chip technology domestically](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/taiwan/archives/2026/07/18/2003860953). This strategic "silicon shield" line in the sand ensures that next-generation packaging and sub-1nm fabrication capabilities remain in Taiwan, preserving regional tech-infrastructure leverage despite global onshoring pressures.

### 5. Semiconductor Valuation Correction Deepens as AI Spending Anxieties Grow
While structural demand remains robust, the [global chip stock sell-off has deepened](https://www.morningstar.com/news/marketwatch/20260717148/18-tech-stocks-that-have-fallen-at-least-30-during-july). The PHLX Semiconductor Index (SOX) has plunged nearly 21% so far in July, with 18 large-cap tech and semiconductor stocks shedding at least 30% of their value. The sharp correction reflects heightened investor skepticism regarding the exact timeline for Big Tech to monetize its astronomical, multi-billion-dollar infrastructure capex.
